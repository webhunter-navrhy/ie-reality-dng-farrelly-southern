About the property

Description property

DNG Farrelly Southern is delighted to present No. 53 Newtown Court, Maynooth - a well-maintained three-bedroom semi-detached home occupying an enviable end position within this mature and highly convenient residential development.

Situated at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac and overlooking an attractive green area, the property enjoys a particularly appealing setting together with the benefit of a larger-than-average end site. The accommodation is bright and comfortable throughout, complemented by a modern kitchen, refurbished shower room and a valuable range of energy-saving upgrades.

The ground-floor accommodation opens into a welcoming entrance hallway with wood-effect flooring and understairs storage. The living room is bright and inviting, featuring an open fireplace with back boiler, attractive fitted cabinetry and a large window overlooking the front garden.

To the rear is a modern kitchen and dining area fitted with a generous selection of floor and wall units, timber-effect worktops and a central island providing useful additional preparation space and casual seating. Double doors open directly onto the patio and rear garden, creating an excellent connection between the indoor and outdoor living areas.

All kitchen appliances are included in the sale, including the Bosch dishwasher, Zanussi washing machine, Neff oven and extractor fan.

Upstairs, there are three bedrooms, including two comfortable double bedrooms and a third bedroom which would also work well as a nursery, home office or study. Fitted Sliderobes provide excellent storage. The accommodation is completed by a stylish, recently refurbished family shower room featuring a large curved shower enclosure, vanity unit, WC and attractive patterned flooring.

A new hot-water cylinder has also recently been installed in the hot press.

The property has a B energy rating and benefits from solar PV panels, a battery storage system and an EV charging point. Heating is provided by an oil-fired central heating system, while the open fire and back boiler can also heat the radiators and domestic hot water.

Outside, the north-west-facing rear garden is laid out with a lawn, paved patio and an attractive selection of mature shrubs and plants. The generous garden offers plenty of room for outdoor dining, children?s play and gardening and also includes a useful storage shed.

To the side of the property is a covered and enclosed pergola-style area with lighting and electricity. Currently used as a home gym, this highly versatile space could also serve as a workshop, hobby area, play space or additional covered storage.

To the front, a private driveway provides convenient off-street parking. The property is also fitted with an alarm system.

Newtown Court is a mature and popular residential development within easy reach of Maynooth town centre, Maynooth University, local primary and secondary schools, shops, cafés, restaurants and sporting amenities. Maynooth Train Station and regular bus services are readily accessible, while the nearby M4 motorway provides convenient access to Dublin and the wider motorway network.

With its excellent end-of-cul-de-sac position, green outlook, generous site, B energy rating and adaptable side space, No. 53 offers a particularly attractive opportunity for first-time buyers, families and those seeking a comfortable home in a well-established Maynooth neighbourhood.

Furniture may also be available as part of the sale, subject to separate agreement.

Viewing is highly recommended.
